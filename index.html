Digital Culture Commons

A way to share and comment on our culture as we would like.

DCC 1.0

SC - Safe Content, It's safe from inappropriate things like nsfw, for share on our digital culture.

ROATW - Respect others as they want, respect others as others want.

DNDPRI - Don't Discuss of Politic and Religious Issues, Don't talk about politics and religious Issues, it can generate controversy.

RP - Respect Privacy, Private information cannot be leaked, privacy is respected.

GF - Groomer-free, Remember to report, show evidence of someone who is manipulating you, we will remove the groomers to make it a safe community

Deed

If a community has these DCC rules and the moderators, including the creator, are responsible for muting, isolating, kicking, and banning users who do not respect the rules, and the moderators are moderating that to make it a safe community, it is approved.

Developer

You can help with things like changing the web style or report any problem and we will see how to solve it on Github Issues and Github Pull Requests. You can also create, for example, bots that if the moderators see that the community server is safe, they can say so, and on disboard, which is a bot to discover and enter Discord servers, let's suppose it is added to the "DCC Approved" list so that there you would know which server you could enter and not have problems.

Note: You can add the commons deed on the Code of conduct of your projects or community as rules
